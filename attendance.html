ℹ️ How QR Check-In Works

  1. Select the service date and type, then click Generate QR Code
  2. Display the QR code on a screen or print it at the church entrance
  3. Members scan the QR with their phone camera — it opens a simple check-in page
  4. They pick their name and tap Check In
  5. Their name appears in the Live Check-Ins feed in real time
  6. Click Apply QR Check-Ins to Attendance to mark them as Present in the attendance list
  7. You can still manually toggle anyone who didn't scan
