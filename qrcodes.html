📖 Step-by-Step for Sundays

1
Print or display this QR code at the church entrance — on a banner, a screen, or a printed A4 sheet.
2
Members scan the QR code with their phone camera (no app needed). It opens the check-in page in their browser.
3
They type their name, see it in the list, and tap it. A ✅ confirmation appears and they're marked present.
4
Ushers help elderly members or visitors by doing the same on their phone — search the name, tap, done.
5
Visitors use the "I am a Visitor" section to enter their name and check in without needing to be in the member list.
6
Attendance syncs automatically — open the Attendance page to see all check-ins and submit the final record.
